# Assignment 3 Specification

## Purpose

This assignment makes sure that you understand a large variety of Australian International Trade datasets and data types. You learn statistical and analytical patterns in data. You use different visualisation techniques to present these patterns and to uncover data stories inside the dataset.

## Weighting

40%

## Due date

25 Oct 2026 at 23:59

## Task

Write a report for readers in public policy circles or government. The report summarises key findings about Australian trade data. Look at more than 10 years of trade data with a variety of analysis patterns.

### Requirements

1. Create the analytical and statistical patterns in Excel. Load them into the dashboard tool.
2. Create visualisations that analyse and compare all main categories. Cover the raw dollar value trends, the analytical trends and the statistical trends.
3. Analyse the relationship between the 10 main categories. Identify the major trends and the change points for each year. Explain the possible causes of the trends you observe. Use both the given data and research from outside the data.
4. Analyse the relationship between the main category and the subcategories through the visualisation. Identify the major trends and the change points. Explain the possible causes of the trends you observe from within the given data.
5. Focus on ONE subcategory. Create one or more dashboards that look into the trends, the insights for each year, and the relationship with another subcategory.
6. Create one or more storyboards. The storyboards summarise the stories about ONE subcategory. They also compare the trends and change points of other subcategories over time.
7. Pick a product from the dashboard and the storyboard. Choose either import or export for that product. Visualise the trends of the ten years between 2016 and 2025. State what you gain or lose across those ten years. State your target for the next five years, between 2026 and 2030. Show those trends and the forecast with storytelling visualisations. Use AI to analyse the same product. The AI compares the trends between 2016 and 2025 and forecasts between 2026 and 2030. Compare the details of your data storyboard against the AI outcomes. Explain and summarise the advantages and disadvantages of AI.
8. Write a report that explains what you found in your data visualisation process. Describe the graphical techniques you used. Highlight and compare the trends and the outliers with storytelling graphs. Analyse why they may have happened, with a focus on your selected category and subcategories. Present the visual graphs that support your comparisons, insights and storytelling. Conclude with recommendations about the selected industries. Summarise the advantages and disadvantages of the dashboard and storyboard methods.

The marking rubric is in the section [Marking rubric](#marking-rubric).

## The dataset

The Australian International Trade dataset from ABS Statistics holds more than 30 years of data, between 1988 and 2025. The dataset for this assignment holds import and export information from 2016 to 2025. It has 10 main categories and 67 subcategories. Each subcategory covers the performance of several industries on productivity and resources.

The dataset file is `2026-Spring-Ass3-V20.xlsx`.

## Submission

The submission has three files:

1. A report in PDF format, named `Student_ID_A3.pdf`.
2. The Excel file you used, named `Student_ID_A3.xlsx`. Submit it whether you edited it or not.
3. The dashboard workbook you used. The brief asks for a packaged Tableau file, named `Student_ID_A3.twbx`.

The packaged format stores the data with the workbook. The marker then does not need a link to your Excel workbook.

The report has a limit of 20 pages. Graphs count towards the limit.

## What the rubric adds to the task list

The rubric states three points that the task list does not make clear:

1. Every criterion asks for import AND export. The Excel patterns, the main to subcategory analysis, the dashboard and the storyboard all need both directions.
2. Criterion 1 accepts "Tableau or other apps". A Python dashboard meets this criterion.
3. Criterion 3 marks the visual layout, the labels and the colour patterns. Craft carries marks, not only the analysis.

## Deviation from the brief

This project does not use Tableau. The dashboards and storyboards are built as a Python dashboard instead. Requirements 1, 5, 6 and 7 apply to the Python dashboard. The third submission file is the dashboard source, not a `.twbx` file.

Excel stays the home of the analytical and statistical patterns. The spreadsheet and the PDF are the marked artefacts. No result may live only in Python code.
